THE OREGON TRAIL

Lead your party from Independence, Missouri, to the Willamette Valley
in Oregon, 2040 miles away. Each turn is one week of travel or rest.

Pick a pace and rations, hunt with your bullets, and buy oxen, food,
clothing, ammunition, and spare wagon parts at the trading posts.
Watch your health: illness, snakebites, and bad weather can kill.
Cross rivers by ford, caulk, or ferry. The party leader must survive.
